About me

Beyond the application.

I originally imagined my future in medicine because I wanted to do work that helps people. My path eventually shifted toward technology, but the motivation behind it stayed the same: understand problems, find practical solutions and build things that make a real difference.

During my Software Engineering studies, I became increasingly interested in what happens beyond the application itself — how systems are deployed, connected, monitored, tested and automated. That naturally led me toward Linux, networking, cloud infrastructure and DevOps.

What I enjoy most is understanding how different technical layers interact. I like building environments, testing them, troubleshooting problems and figuring out why something behaves the way it does. Today, my main focus is DevOps and Cloud Engineering, while I continue strengthening my foundations in software engineering and networking.

My goal is to become an engineer who can understand a system end to end — from application code to networking and cloud infrastructure — and build solutions that are reliable, maintainable and well understood.

Growing up across different languages and environments has also shaped the way I communicate and approach problems from different perspectives.
